Experience

Camera-based Individual Monitoring in Agriculture

PhD researcher, ML/CV · NWO project IMAGEN 2022.1 – 2026.9 TU Eindhoven, NL
Technical contributions
  • Built a multi-object pig tracking pipeline benchmarked on 22K+ annotated frames, reaching MOTA 88.6% at 48.9 FPS with re-ID association.
  • Built keypoint-guided spatiotemporal models (SlowFast, Transformer) for ear- and tail-biting recognition; a head-centric input representation with geometric correction raised Top-1 accuracy from 62.5% to 77.3%, validated by ablation studies.
  • Built automated Python data pipelines over large-scale video — frame extraction, quality filtering and dataset construction — designed for reliability and reproducibility.
  • Ran containerized multi-GPU processing on HPC (Snellius / SURF) with SLURM batch jobs and automated experiment configuration and tracking.
  • Designed a multi-camera tracking strategy to strengthen identity association across views.
Collaboration & teaching
  • Worked in the NWO IMAGEN consortium with Wageningen University, Utrecht University and industry partners Topigs Norsvin and Hendrix Genetics on commercial-scale agricultural AI.
  • Turned open-ended requirements from farm and industry partners into concrete, testable tasks with defined metrics and data-quality criteria.
  • Supervised a master internship and a master thesis on computer vision for livestock monitoring — both successfully defended.
  • Gave guest lectures and assisted assignments on object detection and tracking in Wageningen Institute of Animal Sciences (WIAS) courses.

Video-based Animal Detection and Tracking

Master thesis · TU/e 2021.2 – 2021.11 Eindhoven, NL
  • Built and annotated multi-class pig and laying-hen video datasets (thousands of frames); wrote annotation tutorials and ran workshops so annotators shared one labelling standard.
  • Benchmarked one-shot and two-stage detection and tracking methods, and proposed an identity association algorithm that reduces identity switches.

Atrial Fibrillation Classification Using CNNs

Master internship · TU/e 2020.7 – 2020.8 Eindhoven, NL
  • Explored CNNs for atrial fibrillation classification on graph representations of ECG recordings, converting 1D signals to 2D via STFT, MSE and wavelet transforms.
